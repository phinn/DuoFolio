DuoFolio

A spread reader built natively for iPhone Duo's fold

The whole book flows across both displays as one continuous canvas — unfold for a true two-page spread, fold shut and the cover display becomes your bookmark.


Get it on the App Store

01True two-page spread

Reads like a real book.

Hold iPhone Duo sideways and a book opens into a genuine spread — even pages on the left, odd pages on the right, with a soft paper gutter between them. DuoFolio turns whole leaves, not half screens.

02Cover-display bookmark

Fold Duo shut and the outer display becomes a bookmark card — cover art, title and progress, glanceable without opening the phone. Flip it open and reading resumes on the exact column you left.

03Fully local

Imported EPUBs never leave the device. No account, no tracking, no network. Reading position, theme and type size are stored on-device only — your books stay yours.

Built by a solo indie developer to answer one question: what should reading feel like on a device that folds like a book?

Four languages — Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese. Warm paper tones, serif body text, and a layout engine that follows the reader across folded, half-open and fully open states.
