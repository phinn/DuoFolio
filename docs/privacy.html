DuoFolio

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2026-10-17

Zero upload. By design.

DuoFolio is a fully local EPUB reader built for iPhone Duo. Importing, parsing, pagination and reading all happen entirely on your device. Your books never leave your hands. There is no server communication, no account, no analytics, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. DuoFolio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Where your data lives

  • Imported books: inside the app's sandbox on your device (app group container)
  • Reading progress, bookmarks and highlights: local database on your device
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything at once

Network access

None. DuoFolio performs no network requests of any kind.
