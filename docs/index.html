DuoFolio

A two-page reader built for the iPhone Duo fold

The book flows across both halves of the inner display as one continuous spread — unfold for a real two-page layout, fold shut and the cover display becomes your bookmark. Your library never leaves the device.

01A real spread, not a stretched page

Hold the Duo open and a book opens with it.

Even pages on the left, odd pages on the right, with a soft paper gutter running down the hinge. DuoFolio turns whole leaves — it does not simply pour one column of text across a wider screen and call it a spread.

02Closing the phone becomes a bookmark

Fold the Duo shut and the outer display turns into a cover card — cover art, title, and how far you have read, all glanceable without opening the phone. Flip it open and reading resumes on the exact column you left, whether the phone is folded, half-open, or fully unfolded.

03Your library stays on the device

Imported EPUBs are never uploaded. There is no account to create, no analytics to opt out of, and no network dependency. Reading position, theme and type size live on the device only.

Why this matters for a reader: a reading app sees everything you read. DuoFolio is built so there is nothing to collect in the first place, rather than a promise to handle your data carefully.

04Typography for long sessions

Serif body faces, tuned line spacing, and paper-tone themes built for hours rather than minutes. Four interface and book languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese.

Built by a solo indie developer to answer one question: what should reading feel like on a device that folds like a book?

DuoFolio is written around the fold rather than merely running on it. If you are weighing it against the other options, the honest comparison is on the next page — including the cases where a free app is the better choice.
