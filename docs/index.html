DuoFolio

An EPUB reader built for the iPhone Duo fold

The book flows across both halves of the inner display as one continuous spread — unfold for a real two-page layout, fold shut and the cover display becomes your bookmark. Your library never leaves the device. Import your own DRM-free EPUB or PDF files and open them straight into a spread.

01A real spread, not a stretched page

Hold the Duo open and a book opens with it.

Even pages on the left, odd pages on the right, with a soft paper gutter running down the hinge. DuoFolio turns whole leaves — it does not pour one column of text across a wider screen and call it a spread.

02The page turns like paper

Catch the corner, and the leaf lifts with your finger.

DuoFolio does not slide or fade between pages. The leaf curls — the corner rolls up, the back of the page catches the light, its shadow falls across the gutter — and the next leaf is already underneath, waiting. Drag it part way and it hangs there; let go and it settles.

It is the one thing a foldable can do that a flat phone cannot: a page that behaves like a page, on a device that opens like a book.

03Closing the phone becomes a bookmark

Fold the Duo shut and the outer display turns into a cover card — cover art, title, and how far you have read, all glanceable without opening the phone. Flip it open and reading resumes on the exact column you left, whether the phone is folded, half-open, or fully unfolded.

04Your library stays on the device

Imported EPUBs are never uploaded. There is no account to create, no analytics to opt out of, and no network dependency. Reading position, theme and type size live on the device only.

Why this matters for a reader: a reading app sees everything you read. DuoFolio is built so there is nothing to collect in the first place, rather than a promise to handle your data carefully.

05Typography for long sessions

Serif body faces, tuned line spacing, and paper-tone themes built for hours rather than minutes. On a larger screen the measure widens rather than stretching — the same layout rules hold on an iPad as on the Duo.

Nine languages

English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Español and Português (Brasil) — the interface and the App Store listing, not just the menu.

Built by a solo indie developer to answer one question: what should reading feel like on a device that folds like a book?
