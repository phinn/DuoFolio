# DuoFolio

A two-page EPUB and PDF reader built for the iPhone Duo fold.

**Site:** https://phinn.github.io/DuoFolio/
**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823855712

Unfold the Duo and a book opens into a real spread — even pages on the left, odd pages on the right,
with a paper gutter at the hinge. Fold it shut and the outer display becomes a cover card showing
title and progress. Imported books stay on the device: no account, no upload, no analytics.
